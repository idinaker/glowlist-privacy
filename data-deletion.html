GlowList – Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users of GlowList can delete their local data, cloud backup data, and associated Google Sign-In account record.

1. Deleting Local Device Data

All lists, tasks, reminders, and settings stored on your device can be deleted immediately at any time:

  1. Swipe left on any list or task item inside GlowList to delete individual entries, or
  2. Open Android Settings → Apps → GlowList → Storage & cache → Clear storage (or uninstall the app) to permanently erase all local data on your device.

2. Requesting Account & Cloud Backup Deletion

If you used the optional Google Sign-In and Cloud Backup features and wish to permanently delete your Firebase Authentication account record and all cloud-stored lists and items (`users/{uid}/lists` and `users/{uid}/items`):

  1. Open the GlowList Google Play Store listing page and locate the Developer Contact / Support Email, or open an issue at https://github.com/idinaker.
  2. Send a message with the subject line "GlowList Account Deletion Request" from the Google email address associated with your GlowList sign-in.
  3. We will permanently delete your authentication record and all associated Cloud Firestore backup documents within 7 business days (and no later than 30 days) and send you confirmation once completed.

3. What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted: Your Firebase Authentication user profile (Name, Email, UID) and all backed-up lists and task items stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Retained: No personal or task data is retained by us after deletion. Standard financial transaction records for any Google Play subscriptions are maintained directly by Google Play in accordance with tax and accounting regulations.
